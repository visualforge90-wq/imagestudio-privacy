## v1.0 — first release
Welcome to Image Studio! Edit photos with filters, adjustments, crop with social-ready
sizes, blur/sharpen/vignette, and one-tap Auto-enhance. Remove backgrounds with on-device
AI. Make collages (grids and shaped layouts), side-by-side comparisons, and freeform
canvases with photos, text, stickers and drawing. Export at exact sizes for Instagram,
Stories, YouTube and more. Private and offline-first.
