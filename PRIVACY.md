# Image Studio — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

Image Studio is an **offline-first** photo editor. Your photos are yours. This policy
explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

## The short version

- **Your photos never leave your device.** All editing, collage, side-by-side, and
  freeform compositing happens locally. We do not upload, transmit, or store your images
  on any server. We have no server.
- **We don't collect analytics or personal data.** The app has no account system and no
  tracking SDK.
- The app requests **no INTERNET permission of its own.**

## What the app accesses on your device

- **Photos you pick.** Via the Android Photo Picker / system picker, only for images you
  explicitly select. Saved projects keep a private copy of those images inside the app's
  own storage so you can reopen them; these copies are removed when you delete the project.
- **Saving to your gallery.** When you export, the finished image is written to your
  device's shared media (gallery) through MediaStore.

## On-device AI (background removal)

The "Remove background" feature uses **Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation**, which runs
**on your device**. The segmentation model is downloaded once through Google Play Services
the first time you use the feature; after that it works offline. The image is processed
locally and is **not** sent to Google or anyone else for this feature. Google Play
Services is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Ads and purchases

The published build may include:

- **Ads** (Google AdMob) to support development. When enabled, ad serving and consent are
  handled by Google's SDKs, subject to
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). In regions that require
  it, you'll be asked for consent, which you can change any time under **Settings → Manage
  ad privacy**.
The default build ships with these disabled (no network calls).

## Children

Image Studio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Your choices

- Delete any saved project at any time from **Recent Projects**.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally-stored copies of your images.
- Change ad-consent choices under **Settings**.
